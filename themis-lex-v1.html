Where AI Must Not Touch

3 workflows

Reviewing sealed juvenile dependency filings

Rule
Sealed juvenile records carry strict confidentiality protections and may not leave court-controlled systems under any circumstance.
Risk
Ingesting sealed content into any third-party model is a reportable confidentiality breach, not a procedural lapse.
Instead
Route to the supervising clerk. Use the internal review template already approved by court IT.

Drafting bench memoranda for a pending ruling

Rule
Substantive judicial work product must reflect the judicial officer's own reasoning. Drafting analysis on their behalf crosses an ethical line, not a procedural one.
Risk
AI-drafted analysis embedded in a bench memo risks undetected hallucinated citations reaching a ruling.
Instead
Continue current practice — research attorney prepares the memo; JA formats and distributes.

Summarizing victim statements for a sentencing file

Rule
Victim communications carry dignitary protections beyond standard PII. The specific language a victim chose is part of the record, not a draft to be summarized.
Risk
Condensation can strip the specific language a victim chose — a harm the summary format cannot undo.
Instead
Preserve statements verbatim. If length is an issue, discuss with the judicial officer before editing.
